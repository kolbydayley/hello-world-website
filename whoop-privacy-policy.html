WHOOP Integration Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-19

This policy describes how the personal WHOOP integration used by Kolby Dayley accesses and handles WHOOP data.

Data we access

  • Recovery metrics (e.g., recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate)
  • Strain/cycle metrics
  • Sleep metrics (e.g., duration, sleep performance)
  • Body measurements made available by WHOOP API scopes

How data is used

  • To generate daily readiness and training recommendations in a private health system.
  • To support personal analytics and trend tracking.
  • To improve workout planning and recovery decisions.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored in private infrastructure controlled by the account owner.
  • OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in secure credential storage.
  • No data is sold.

Sharing

WHOOP data is not shared with third parties for marketing or resale. Data is used only for personal health analysis and related automations requested by the account owner.

Retention

Data is retained as needed for ongoing personal trend analysis and can be deleted by the account owner from the local system.
